# Uncrowned — what v3 is

> **Read this first.** `V2.md` says what the game became on the evening of 2026-09-13;
> this says what it is since the next morning, when the world under it changed.
> `SPECS.md` answers *what the game should be*, except where the simulation's redesign of
> 2026-09-18 supersedes it (`SIMULATION_MODEL.md` and the three documents CLAUDE.md names);
> this answers *what it is*, which is the question you have if you have just arrived.
> Delivered 2026-09-13 through 2026-09-15: M1a–c, M2a–b, M3a and the M4 cut-over of
> `MIGRATION_3D.md` — its own phases, not `DEMO_TASKS.md`'s M group. **What has been built
> since is in *Since the map* below; where the work stands today is CLAUDE.md's table.**

**The game plays on the world Yannick's brother is building in 3D, and shows nothing
but his.** His terrain, water, forests and burnt Brindle are the ground; the simulation
still thinks in tiles, because his 768 m are baked into a 384 × 384 grid at two metres a
tile. Every person is his traveller sprite until he draws the cast; every building his
library holds is his mesh; what he has not drawn yet stands as a plain block in his
rock paint, and the woods and roads the simulation has and he has not are simply not
there to see — his to plant and lay. Nothing of the 2D game's art is on screen (the
HUD's font aside). The map changed nothing about *what the game is* — and four days
later the simulation did: since 2026-09-18 a place has two values and the kingdom two
(`SIMULATION_MODEL.md`), and the twelve quantities, rank and the three routes to the king
are on their way out (group C of `DEMO_TASKS.md`).

The game is written and played in **French first** and translated into English.

## Run it

```bash
godot --headless --path . --import          # once after cloning, and after a new class_name
tools/vendor_workshop.sh                    # once after cloning, and after each delivery of his
tools/run_tests.sh                          # fast suite, ~25 s, on the baked world
tools/run_tests.sh --all                    # everything, ~63 s — before committing
tools/run_tests.sh --procedural --all       # the same suite on the 2D map, ~55 s — before committing too
godot --path . &                            # play it, in 3D; =/- or the wheel zooms
tools/shot.sh /tmp/a.png play 292,290       # one frame of the 3D window, standing at a tile
UNCROWNED_VIEW=2d tools/shot.sh /tmp/b.png map                   # the baked world, flat
godot --headless --path . -s tools/bake_region.gd                # his data + the brief -> content/region.json
godot --headless --path . -s tools/measure_routes.gd             # the road and the wild, at his pace
godot --headless --path . -s tools/play_opening.gd               # the first fifteen minutes, headless
tools/opening_frames.sh /tmp/opening fr en                       # the opening's frames, both languages
godot --headless --path . -s tools/map_criteria.gd               # MAP_SPEC's twelve, on the baked grid
```

`--headless` never calls `_draw()`, so the suite cannot see the screen. Every visual
claim below was checked with a picture.

At the cut-over: **43 suites, 431 tests**, green on both worlds, nine DEBT lines for
five claims. **Today (2026-09-30, after group T): 63 suites, 737 tests**, green on both worlds; the baked
run prints nine DEBT lines for seven claims and three OFF lines (the list is below).

**The ironworks and crossings delivery is ingested (2026-09-15, PR #6).** The 27
buildings and 47 props replace the Cinderworks kit; its zone follows the delivered
100 × 104 m bounds. Collision shapes, extracted outside core, keep exterior approaches
and open halls separate from roof envelopes. All five bridges are named and joined by
the delivered regional roads. The window seats walkers on the bridge decks and cools
the delivered furnace and forge effects when the works is freed. Harry and the
watchmen stand by a kiln through feature anchors; the bridge guard stands on its deck.
The generated scene copy is 1,507 files, 25.4 MB. Town and bridge frames were inspected.

**His second delivery is ingested (2026-09-16).** The factory is his v4 layout: the
six furnaces stand in an ordered three-by-two grid, sixteen of the twenty-seven
buildings moved — `DortoirNord` right across the site — and the works' zone follows
his widened 110 m bounds, its centre now (312,213). The delivered town is read by his
building ids, so none of that cost content a line. Seven of his crossings are named
and joined, including `PontPorteRoyale` over his new royal moat, whose deck the window
now seats walkers on.

**His sawmill village and his royal mountain city are on screen and not yet in the
simulation.** Fifteen buildings with a working mill wheel and its water channel;
thirty-four buildings, a curtain wall of eleven towers, a switchback climb, a stone
bridge and a moat. They arrive with his own map plate, so the window draws them —
but `village_scierie` is still ignored by the brief and Cairnwell still carries our
scaffold kit on top of his city, the HUD calls the sawmill *les terres sauvages*, and
his walls stop nobody. Reading `sawmill-town.json` and `royal-city.json` the way
`ironworks-town.json` is read is the next piece of ingestion, one apiece; his geography
now names both with `built_scene`, `built_bounds_xz` and `built_buildings`. **No task
lists it yet**, and `MIGRATION_3D.md` §9 decided on 2026-09-13 — before he built it —
that the sawmill village goes; the brief still ignores `village_scierie`. Whether it is
wanted is Yannick's to say.

**And one rule of the bake changed.** Once he delivered any crossing, the bake took
every wet road tile to be one of his bridges. His royal feeder channel crossed the
King's Road twenty-five tiles from his nearest bridge, three water tiles went down
in silence, and the road to the capital was cut — the King's Road's waypoints jumped
112 tiles in one leg and a walker spent 400 s covering 284 tiles. His bridges run
before the roads, so a tile still wet under a road is one no bridge of his covers:
the bake lays it and the report names it. Beside his own deck it still refuses.

## Since the map: what the game grew (2026-09-18 → 29)

The simulation was redesigned on 2026-09-18 and rebuilt task by task
(`docs/DEMO_TASKS.md`; CLAUDE.md's table says which are built):

- **A place has two values, the kingdom two** (M), people with routines who leave a
  town that stops (P), and **the demo's quest at the Cinderworks** — workers against
  management, one act at the furnaces, a works that visibly runs or stops (Q).
- **The player has a simulation of his own** (J): an allégeance he chooses, a standing
  per town that moves only with witnessed deeds, a richesse that is gold.
- **A turn-based fight on the world grid** (K, `docs/COMBAT_V2.md`), in place, the camera
  dropped and never turned; the first, real-time design was played, rejected and deleted
  (F, K6). **Wolves** hold the demo's road before the bridge (W).
- **The shell** (S1–S2): four traits, a pool of 8, and a public build that opens on the
  title. **The LLM layer is deleted** (C1): every line is hand-written.

## The opening, redone (group O, 2026-09-29)

Yannick asked for the first ten to fifteen minutes to be finished work, and they were
rebuilt as twenty-two steps (`docs/DEMO_TASKS.md`, group O). **The player wakes in
Brindle's cemetery**, at the burned village's south edge: his fence and gate, his
earth and fallow over the graves, stone steles and wooden planks made in his materials,
and the fairy beside a campfire composed from his pieces — the first save. **Walking
north, Bram calls you over**: a '!' over him, the player held, Bram walking down to
stand beside you, the conversation opening by itself, once — a `Hail` store and its
system, two discs of ground found by search so that every shortest way into the
village crosses them. **He teaches three drills** on the world grid: the sword, the bow
— Wren hands you one and you shoot Bram as he comes at you — and magic with the fairy's
gift, the one spell in the game. Then the road north, two wolves before the bridge, and the works'
gate. `tools/play_opening.gd` plays all of it headless, stage by stage, replays it and
reloads its save, and prints what each stage cost; the frames of O21 are the check a
suite cannot make, and Yannick playing it is the one that counts.

## After his play (group T, 2026-09-29)

He played it the same evening, and the combat tutorial was the finding: he could not
shoot, nothing said why, and an arrow could always be dodged. **An arrow now lands when
it is shot**, for everybody, from two tiles to six and never the next one; Bram's bow line
**hands you a bow of your own**, **U changes the weapon in your hands**, and the keys line
says what K does with what you hold. Each lesson's card lists **its steps with their keys**
and a **hint that answers where you stand** — and the first round of every lesson, which
had been drawing no keys at all, draws them. **Attacking the works' gatekeeper** brings
three king's guards, far stronger than anybody else (group V, 2026-09-30); by a miracle the
four can be beaten, the gate is taken, and the furnaces are the player's — putting one out
brings more guards, lighting one brings Tom. The fairies' clearing is
gone from the map, and the fast suite runs in about 25 s instead of 41. `docs/DEMO_TASKS.md`,
group T, has each step, and a fresh-eyes review walked it: the gatekeeper's post is kept manned, so his ward is never a wall nobody can see.

## The debts the suite prints

Seven claims, nine DEBT lines. Four are the map's and the brief's:

- **The furnaces are 80 tiles north of Brindle's centre**, to the works' nearest edge; §4
  wants them in Brindle's first frame. His to move or the narrative's to adapt — Yannick's spec-review lane.
- **The full road length takes 147 s at his pace**; §4's 45–90 s band was settled for six
  tiles a second and is renegotiated with the map, not defended.
- **The road ratio is 1.24**, below §4's 1.30–1.50 target. The delivered bridge
  connections shorten the route; its shape is the brother's and Yannick's to settle.
- **The north his rivers close** (2026-09-14, his rivers v4). Two new tributaries cut off
  a stretch of wild north of the castle, 15 percent of the passable map, with no
  crossing over either; nothing of the cast stands in it. His bridge to add, or
  wilderness nobody needs.

And three are **things his brother has not drawn**, each stood in by something of ours
under the art rule's exceptions: a blow (the fight frames), a beast (the wolf), a grave
(the cemetery's markers).

A DEBT is printed with the run and counted apart from failures so the suite stays green
while the brief (`MIGRATION_3D.md` §5) is open. Never a failure turned into a debt to get
green; a debt names something a person has to settle.

## What is deliberately not there

- **The wild's price in time, and the music** — switched off for now (Yannick,
  2026-09-14, walking his brother's map: *slow off the path is useless; remove the music
  as well*). `Region.TERRAIN_SLOWS_YOU` and `Sound.MUSIC`, one word each; the tables
  stand until C4 removes them, and two tests say `OFF` in the run (a third `OFF` is the
  2D map's road into Brindle, which the baked world does not claim).
- **His art in place of the blocks** (M3b): tents, boats, stalls, papers,
  the counting house, the keep and towers, the gatehouses, the muster rolls; the
  free-state variants as real scenes; travellers as carts; the sign as a prop at each
  gate; and **the 25 faces** in his style — the one asset every conversation stands in
  front of. Until then everyone is his traveller and twenty-five people share one face,
  while the Cinderworks now has its own furnaces and forges.
- **The map's fill** (M5): Harrowgate, the Muster, Saltmarch, the fields, Cairnwell's
  streets and the castle stand as our kit on his ground; the Thornwood is the brief's;
  the five delivered bridges and their roads are now ingested. The northern tributaries
  still leave wilderness disconnected.
- **The provenance-and-licence validator** for 3D assets and §13 rewritten around it
  wait for the assets they concern.
- **The canopy fading over the player**: the brief's trees are one mesh; his trees will
  do it.
- **A fight with the king.** Touching him still kills you on the third touch, v1's
  contact rule; the turn-based duel never took him on, and nothing retires the rule
  until the way to the king is designed (CLAUDE.md, *Standing exceptions*).

## The debug tools

Thirteen, all gated on `OS.has_feature("debug")` and all described in CLAUDE.md
(*Development tools that must never ship*): `T` skips a day · `G` makes you unkillable ·
the journal's last page lists who is where · `tools/shot.sh` photographs any screen, with
`UNCROWNED_FREE`, `_TOWN`, `_LENS`, `_DUEL`, `_TALK`, `_DID`, `_QUICK`, `_HAIL` and `_FACTS` to set up
the frame. `M`, the map of Erileo, began as one and ships as a feature. Two selectors that
are not debug tools and not gated: `UNCROWNED_WORLD=procedural` (the 2D map) and `UNCROWNED_VIEW=2d`
(the baked world, flat).
