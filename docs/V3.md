# Uncrowned — what v3 is

> **Read this first.** `V2.md` says what the game became on the evening of 2026-09-13;
> this says what it is since the next morning, when the world under it changed.
> `SPECS.md` is the source of truth and answers *what the game should be*; this answers
> *what it is*, which is the question you have if you have just arrived. Delivered
> 2026-09-13 and 2026-09-14: M1a–c, M2a–b, M3a and the M4 cut-over of `MIGRATION_3D.md`.

**The game plays on the world Yannick's brother is building in 3D.** His terrain,
water, roads, forests and burnt Brindle are the ground; the simulation still thinks in
tiles, because his 768 m are baked into a 384 × 384 grid at two metres a tile; the
people, the fires and the market stalls are the pixel figures v1 and v2 drew, stood up
on his ground until he draws the cast in his style. Nothing about *what the game is*
changed: the five pillars, the twelve quantities, hardship, the four places and their
two states, rank, the readings, the endings, the journal, French first.

The game is written and played in **French first** and translated into English.

## Run it

```bash
godot --headless --path . --import          # once after cloning, and after a new class_name
tools/vendor_workshop.sh                    # once after cloning, and after each delivery of his
tools/run_tests.sh                          # fast suite, ~8 s, on the baked world
tools/run_tests.sh --all                    # everything, ~24 s — before committing
tools/run_tests.sh --procedural --all       # the same suite on the 2D map, ~20 s — before committing too
godot --path . &                            # play it, in 3D
tools/shot.sh /tmp/a.png play 292,290       # one frame of the 3D window, standing at a tile
UNCROWNED_VIEW=2d tools/shot.sh /tmp/b.png map                   # the baked world, flat
godot --headless --path . -s tools/bake_region.gd                # his data + the brief -> content/region.json
godot --headless --path . -s tools/measure_routes.gd             # road / wild / attuned, at his pace
godot --headless --path . -s tools/map_criteria.gd               # MAP_SPEC's twelve, on the baked grid
```

`--headless` never calls `_draw()`, so the suite cannot see the screen. Every visual
claim below was checked with a picture.

## What v3 built

| Phase | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **M1a — anchors** | Where everything stands is data: `content/places.json` holds the eight places, the clearing, the crossings, and an anchor — a place or point plus an offset — for every person, fire, stall and paper. No `.gd` and no cast sheet carries a tile. `test_anchors` fails **by name** on an anchor that resolves nowhere | `core/places.gd`, `Region.resolve`, `content/places.json` |
| **M1b — the bake** | His four landscape files, his geography, his sectors and trees plus `content/bake_brief.json` — our proposals, in his metres, for what his data lacks — become `content/region.json`: terrain kinds, his buildings, the places, the points, the pace. Deterministic (baked twice, compared), hashed, checked in, `--check` in CI | `core/rules/bake_rules.gd`, `core/region_bake.gd`, `tools/bake_region.gd` |
| **M1c — the kit** | The procedural map's settlement stamps became a kit (`Region.scaffold_place`) the bake stands on his terrain for every place he has not built; the brief plants the Thornwood, the fields and the marsh; the road follows his road tiles; the suites say where they stand in the world's terms. A claim the map owes is a **DEBT**, printed and counted apart | `core/region.gd`, `test/test_case.gd`, `tools/test_runner.gd` |
| **M2a — the window** | `view/world3d.gd`: his ground as meshes coloured by the baked terrain, the pixel figures as billboards, his camera in numbers, the fairy as light. The play screen is the bridge: it hands the window one frame dictionary a tick and the window writes nothing back | `view/world3d.gd`, `view/main.gd` |
| **M2b — his scenes** | `tools/vendor_workshop.gd` copies his project's folders into `view3d/workshop/` with their `res://` paths repointed — generated, never committed. The window adopts his map plate (terrain, water, relief, Brindle, forests, mine, sun) and lays the simulation's ground over it as a part-transparent skin; the brief's wood grows everywhere but under his trees; his buildings are his meshes | `tools/vendor_workshop.gd`, `view/world3d.gd` |
| **M3a — parity** | What the 2D window shows that needs none of his art: the marks over whoever can see you, embers over fires and kilns, cold kilns for a freed works, the fairy's glow, the occlusion fade, tents struck, fences gone, the crowd sized, the counting house and the castle darkened by their readings | `view/world3d.gd` |
| **M4 — cut-over** | The baked world is the game; `UNCROWNED_WORLD=procedural` is the 2D map. Walking follows the workshop: 2.5 tiles a second, read from the world's data; six on the 2D map. A save remembers its world | `core/places.gd`, `core/rules/movement_rules.gd`, `core/save_file.gd` |

**42 suites, 425 tests** — green on both worlds. On the baked one the run prints four
DEBT lines, which are two claims: the road band is measured by three suites.

## The numbers, and where they came from

| | 2D map | Baked world | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grid | 280 × 200 | **384 × 384 at 2 m** | His 385-sample grid over 768 m |
| Walking | 6 tiles/s | **2.5 tiles/s** | Decision 1: his controller walks at 5 m/s |
| The King's Road, Brindle to the castle | 58 s | **167 s** | Measured by walking it (`measure_routes`) |
| The wild line | 70 s | **297 s** | The road is still the fast way, the wild the slow one |
| The attuned wild | 67 s | **229 s** | Between the two, as §11 wants |
| MAP_SPEC's criteria | 10 of 10 | **9 of 10** | Criterion 6, the 45–90 s band, was settled for six tiles a second |
| Road ratio | 1.41 | **1.31** | Measured along his road tiles |

## The two debts the suite prints

- **The furnaces are 70 tiles from Brindle's centre**; §4 wants them in Brindle's
  first frame. His to move or the narrative's to adapt — Yannick's spec-review lane.
- **The King's Road takes 155 s at his pace**; §4's 45–90 s band was settled for six
  tiles a second and is renegotiated with the map, not defended.

A DEBT is printed with the run and counted apart from failures so the suite stays green
while the brief (`MIGRATION_3D.md` §5) is open. Never a failure turned into a debt to get
green; a debt names something a person has to settle.

## What is deliberately not there

- **His art in place of the placeholders** (M3b): props by kind from his library, the
  free-state variants as real scenes, travellers as carts, the sign as a prop at each
  gate, and **the 25 faces** in his style — the one asset every conversation stands in
  front of. Until then the pixel figures ride his ground and look like two games glued
  together, on purpose.
- **The map's fill** (M5): Harrowgate, the Muster, Saltmarch, the fields, Cairnwell's
  streets and the castle stand as our kit on his ground; the Thornwood is the brief's;
  four of his roads cross water with no bridge and three stop short of where they go.
  The bake's report names each.
- **The provenance-and-licence validator** for 3D assets and §13 rewritten around it
  wait for the assets they concern.
- **The canopy fading over the player**: the brief's trees are one mesh; his trees will
  do it.
- **Combat**, as in v1 and v2.

## The debug tools, all gated on `OS.has_feature("debug")`

`T` skips an in-game day · `M` opens the map of Erileo, which is now his map · the
journal's last page lists who is where · `tools/shot.sh` photographs any screen ·
`UNCROWNED_FREE=zone[,zone]` frees places for a frame. Two selectors that are not debug
tools and not gated: `UNCROWNED_WORLD=procedural` (the 2D map) and `UNCROWNED_VIEW=2d`
(the baked world, flat).
