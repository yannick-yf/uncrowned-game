# Uncrowned — what v2 is

> **Read this first.** `V1.md` says what was delivered on the morning of 2026-09-13;
> this says what v2 made of it by that evening, and it is the current shape of the
> game. `SPECS.md` is the source of truth and answers *what the game should be*; this
> answers *what it is*, which is the question you have if you have just arrived.
> Delivered 2026-09-13, Phases A–E of SPECS §18.

**The game is not about weakening the king. It is about what the kingdom becomes.**
Breaking it and strengthening it are both real plays, both cost someone, and the ending
is a reading of the state left behind — including the reading where the player is the
one on the throne. v1 could break the kingdom nine ways and build it none. v2 is that
sentence made mechanical, and nothing structural was touched to do it: rows, readings
and words on machinery v1 already had.

The game is written and played in **French first** and translated into English.

---

## Run it

```bash
godot --headless --path . --import          # once after cloning, and after a new class_name
tools/run_tests.sh                          # fast suite, ~7.5 s
tools/run_tests.sh --all                    # everything, ~20 s — before committing
godot --path . &                            # play it
tools/shot.sh /tmp/a.png play 92,150        # look at one frame without playing
UNCROWNED_FREE=wide_acres tools/shot.sh /tmp/b.png play 92,150   # the same, freed
godot --headless --path . -s tools/measure_routes.gd             # road / wild / attuned
```

`--headless` never calls `_draw()`, so the suite cannot see the screen. Every visual
claim below was checked with a picture.

## What v2 built

| Phase | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **A — the wild without teeth** | Beasts gone from the whole map. Terrain speeds on, open country at 0.65. The wild line is the *least-watched* path — it crosses the King's Road where it must and takes the ford — and it is measured: **road 57 s, wild 69 s, attuned wild 66 s**, no blood on any. Travellers are pack horses. Attunement 3+ walks the wood at a field's pace | `core/rules/navigation.gd`, `movement_rules.gd`, `region.gd`, `tools/measure_routes.gd` |
| **B — the second axis** | **Hardship**, per town, beside the twelve: 50 to start, up is worse, moved only by deeds. Every act that moves the kingdom names who it costs (`hardship_effects`), and a test walks the table. Ten **building acts** as spoken deeds to the person whose business it is. A **face** in every place — a greeting that says the place is worse off without naming you | `core/world_tick.gd`, `core/rules/deed_rules.gd`, `core/deeds.gd`, `content/cast.*.json` |
| **C — four places, two states** | The Wide Acres, the Cinderworks, the Muster and the bank are **crown-held or free**, never a third. Freeing is reading the place's thing aloud *there*; holding is one of four spoken acts that need the same thing in hand. A decision holds for **two in-game days** against everything, enforced by not offering the act. The band still moves the two borders. A freed place has no watch. The **entrance sign** speaks in the place's own words | `core/rules/place_rules.gd`, `core/allegiance.gd`, `core/systems/allegiance_system.gd` |
| **D — the crown as a play** | **Rank** is derived from crown standing and falls as well as rises; the last rank is 90 and the gate knows your face there. It changes greetings, never doors. Halgrave carries the king's argument as his second, free question. Deposed at the last rank is the **throne reading** — a reading of one ending, not a sixth — and the journal shows the morning after, town by town | `core/rules/faction_rules.gd`, `core/rules/end_rules.gd`, `core/systems/ending_system.gd` |
| **E — Blackcairn reads the kingdom** | Two derived readings, stored nowhere: **wealth** from treasury, steel and bread; **instability** from how many places changed hands inside one freeze window, whoever moved them. Extra guards on the wall, a dark keep, and three townsfolk who speak of the castle. The journal's **kingdom page** reads it all out in words | `core/rules/castle_rules.gd`, `core/journal.gd`, `view/main.gd` |

**39 suites, 412 tests** — `tools/run_tests.sh --all`. Every phase closed on a playable
proof, listed in SPECS §18's ✅ rows.

## The numbers that changed, and why

| | Was | Is | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open country | 0.80 | **0.65** | Measured: at 0.80 the road beat a plain walker by 2 s and lost to an attuned one |
| The crown's last rank | 110 | **90** | Rank reads standing now, and standing clamps at ±100 |
| The borders' start | neutral | **crown-held** | A place has two states, and a border is the one the band can still move |
| Hardship | — | **50 to start, 62 the line** | One act carries a place past it, so a face can speak the same day |
| The freeze | — | **2 days = 2880 ticks** | Defined once, in `PlaceRules.freeze_ticks()`; also what "recently" means to the castle |
| A crisis | — | **2 flips in a window** | Four in a week is a crisis; the same four over a season is policy |

## What is deliberately not there

- **Combat.** Phase 4 stays out, by decision; the king still kills on contact, and the
  standing exception in CLAUDE.md stands with it.
- **Two of Blackcairn's three ways in.** Only the gate exists, so it stands open. Rank
  changes who says what at it; it never checks it.
- **Greyhold and Harrowgate carry no state.** Deferred, not cut; their v1 rows are kept
  in SPECS §3.
- **Hardship has no drift rule.** It moves only by deeds, which is why it always reads
  as caused. A rate comes with the consequence that needs it, if one ever does.
- **The LLM stays off**, unchanged from v1 and for the same reason (`V1.md`).

## What v2 does not have — the honest gaps

- **The free-state ground is what the pack allows**: fences gone, half the tents, no
  embers, a dark counting house, a dark keep, guards on the wall. Convoys, a queue at
  the granary, shutters, scaffolding and banners wait on a new pack — v3's art pass,
  through the validator (SPECS §13).
- **The entrance sign is a HUD line** while you stand in the place, not a prop at the
  gate. Same reason.
- **The second direction restores; it never raises a full quantity past 100.** Four of
  the twelve start full, so on day one a building act is worth its cost and its rank
  and nothing on the dial. Found by building it, and kept: the crown's acts are worth
  most in a kingdom somebody has already broken.
- **The fast suite is ~7.5 s**, up from 4.7: the place tests each build a full world.
  Still the thing you run without thinking; watch it.
- **No CI, no export preset, no options screen** — as v1.

## The debug tools, all gated on `OS.has_feature("debug")`

`T` skips an in-game day · `M` opens the map of Erileo · the journal's last page lists
who is where · `tools/shot.sh` photographs any screen · **`UNCROWNED_FREE=zone[,zone]`**
frees one or more places for that frame. They are absent from a release export, and
anything else of this kind goes behind the same gate and into CLAUDE.md.
