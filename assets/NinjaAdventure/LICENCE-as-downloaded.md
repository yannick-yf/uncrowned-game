## What this permits

Commercial use, modification, redistribution, and use without attribution. CC0 is
a waiver of copyright rather than a licence with conditions, so there is no notice
requirement to carry forward and no copyleft to propagate.

Attribution is **appreciated but not required**. Uncrowned credits the authors
anyway; see the credits requirement in SPECS §13.
